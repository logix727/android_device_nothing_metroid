## Rejection

The generated `bootclasspath.pb` names
`/system/framework/nt-telephony-interface.jar`, but the install-clean target-files
and installed system omit that jar. The focused explicit module build had staged
it and therefore did not represent full-product membership.

The first airplane-mode enable/disable cycle reproduced the exact r36 fatality:
`com.qti.phone` PID 3425 died in
`QcrilOemhookMsgTunnel.broadcastRrcStateChange()` with
`ClassNotFoundException: com.nothing.telephony.INothingTelephony$Stub`, then
restarted as PID 6745. The remaining nine cycles were stopped immediately.

r37 is rejected and must not be distributed or accepted. The successor must add
the boot jar to product packages and prove target-files plus installed membership
before functional testing.
