## Passed installed gates

- Two coherent r36 boots with distinct boot IDs, one `system_server`, encrypted
  userdata, SELinux Enforcing, expected unlocked/hashtree-disabled AVB state,
  and `sys.boot_completed=1`.
- No new crash-buffer entry, tombstone, or pstore record. The retained tombstone
  timestamps predate r36.
- `vendor.qti.data.factoryservice.IFactory/default` and
  `vendor.qti.hardware.dpmaidlservice.IDpmService/default` register on both
  boots. `cnd` and `vendor.dpmd` remain alive, and QHDC registers after startup.
- Device VoLTE, VT, and WFC IMS capability resources resolve `true` on both
  boots.
- TelephonyProvider materializes and retains all four carrier-ID 1691 rows:
  default/initial-attach, iWLAN MMS, IMS, and XCAP.
- Pinned GMS and Phonesky base packages exist under `/product`; retained updated
  apps remain updated-system packages and GMS services start.

## Not accepted

- No physical SIM or active eSIM subscription was present. Data setup,
  `OEM_DCFAILCAUSE_4`, IMS PDN and registration, calls, SMS, emergency behavior,
  DSDS, and handover remain untested.
- eSIM EID, activation-code download, profile enable, reboot persistence,
  disable, deletion, and physical-SIM coexistence remain untested.

r21 remains the accepted baseline. r36 is an installed coherent candidate with
the bounded integration results above; MTR-001/MTR-002 are not closed.
