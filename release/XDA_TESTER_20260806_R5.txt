INSTALL / UPDATE
1. Use only the matching payload-derived recovery/bootstrap images.
2. In recovery choose Apply update -> Apply from ADB.
3. Run:
     adb sideload lineage-23.0-20260806-UNOFFICIAL-metroid.zip
4. A/B sideload commonly reports about 47 percent on the host before completing;
   wait for recovery to report success.
5. Do not disable AVB/verity and do not manually force a target slot.
