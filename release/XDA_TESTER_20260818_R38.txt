ten airplane-mode cycles. It fixes a deterministic QTI phone crash caused by a
missing stock Nothing telephony boot interface. It also includes the previously
installed IMS device gates, AT&T carrier policy/APNs, complete 3 Sweden
carrier-ID 1691 APNs, and CND/DPM VINTF contracts.
