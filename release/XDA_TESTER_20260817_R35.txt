6. Thermal/charging: controlled unplugged use only; stop and report unexpected
   heat rather than extending the run.

PLAY STORE
- First distinguish a missing/unresolved launcher from certification. If the Play
  Store icon is absent or will not open, use Settings -> Apps -> Google Play Store
  -> Open/Enable once. Do not clear data or remove updates for this test.
- If Google shows "This device isn't Play Protect certified", report
  UNCERTIFIED-BLOCKED. This is expected external policy for the unofficial custom
  build and is not a package crash.
- If the storefront opens, record the exact Settings -> About certification text,
  install one small app and one app larger than 200 MB, reboot and repeat.
- Official private per-device registration may provide REGISTERED-DEVICE ACCESS;
  it does not certify the ROM. Never post Android/GSF ID or account details.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- No physical carrier, camera-finalization, full UDFPS, USB-adjacency,
  thermal/charging, AudioFX-perception or complete face-flow result is claimed.
- Play Protect certification is externally blocked for the unofficial build.
- Unlocked custom-ROM trust state does not provide stock Widevine/Play Integrity
  or HDCP trust.

INSTALLATION
1. Verify the ZIP SHA-256.
2. Existing metroid users should use Lineage Updater/update_engine where available.
3. For recovery sideload, use only this snapshot's bootstrap images, confirm
   recovery success, reboot to the new-slot recovery and sideload the exact GApps
   archive before first Android boot if Google apps are wanted.
4. Do not force a slot, mix images or firmware generations, disable AVB
   verification, or relock around custom images.
