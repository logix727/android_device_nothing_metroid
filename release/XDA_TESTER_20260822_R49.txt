testing. It is not yet the public stable seed.

Identity:

- OTA SHA-256: aa58c5accbade7b491a595398cba028cf90b49f0dafcbcd87ad046f47866351e
- Required baseband on both slots: 1.152387.2.170946.5

Before testing, report `adb shell getprop ro.build.version.incremental` and
`adb shell getprop gsm.version.baseband`. Do not continue on another build or
firmware generation.

Tele2 test:
1. Do not edit APNs or network type.
2. Verify carrier ID 1696 profiles exist for `internet.tele2.se`, `ims`, and `HOS`.
3. Confirm IMS registration.
4. Test one outgoing and incoming call with two-way audio.
5. Reconfirm 5G/data and bidirectional SMS.

Physical SIM2 test:
1. Insert a card in tray position 2.
2. Open the `SIM 2 mode` app and choose `Physical SIM 2`.
3. Confirm the warning and restart.
4. Verify the second subscription appears; test data, SMS and calls.
5. To return to eSIM, choose `eSIM` in the same app, confirm, and restart.

Physical SIM2 and eSIM share slot 1 and cannot be active simultaneously. A stored
profile is not erased by mux switching, but eSIM service is unavailable until
eSIM mode is restored.

Known unrelated issue: the maintainer's current US Mobile eSIM cannot be
disabled/deleted because the eUICC returns GSMA `catBusy`; do not loop erase or
reset operations.
