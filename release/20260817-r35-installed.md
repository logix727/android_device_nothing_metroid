  no new crash, tombstone, pstore, or boot-critical AVC.
- TelephonyProvider checksum migration changed the retained packaged checksum
  and materialized all four carrier-ID 10028 APNs; rows persisted after reboot.
- QTI telephony and IMS package/process/service startup remained stable with both
  physical SIM slots absent.
- Fresh UDFPS enrollment and four captured unlocks passed with zero fingerprint
  HAL deaths. Credential removal later invalidated enrollment as expected.
- NCM local IPv4 passed bidirectionally; Ethernet excluded `usb0`; charging plus
  ADB was restored and `usb0` went down.
- One-shot, prebaked, and primitive haptic requests reached the stable HAL and
  kernel driver.
- One NFC disable/enable cycle returned to on with a stable service PID; known
  transitional ST21 `-107` transport results remained.
- `vendor.gralloc.enable_logs=0`; Aperture preview and multi-camera processing
  ran with stable service PIDs and no Mapper5/GWP-ASan crash or new tombstone.
- A 14-minute-26-second USB-powered screen-off no-SIM gate retained boot ID,
  boot count, and system_server PID with empty crash/pstore gates and no new
  tombstone, fatal, SSR, modem-crash, watchdog-kill, panic, or AVC signature.

## Not accepted

- Physical-SIM/eSIM calls, SMS, data, IMS, emergency, DSDS, and carrier handover.
- Remaining 46 UDFPS unlocks, AOD split, cancellation, and retry matrix.
- NCM IPv6/upstream, cable reconnect, HAL restart, MTP, and RNDIS regressions.
- Trustworthy finalized still/video and MTR-029's ordered ten-cycle camera matrix.
- NFC tag/HCE/payment/off-host and haptic perceived-parity matrices.
- Unplugged deep suspend and long-duration battery-drain acceptance.
- Play Protect certification; the unofficial userdebug build remains externally
  blocked as uncertified under MTR-027. Updated Phonesky initially had a separate
  stopped/unresolved-launcher state; after package-state normalization it launched
  crash-free and Google selected the uncertified-device activity.

r21 remains the accepted baseline. r35 is an installed coherent candidate with
the bounded results above.
